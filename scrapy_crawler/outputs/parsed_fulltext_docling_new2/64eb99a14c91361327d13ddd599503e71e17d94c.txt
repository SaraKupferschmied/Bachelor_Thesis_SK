## Master of Arts Business Communication

## Programme Highlights

Jump into the digital world with a Master that blends communication, strategy, digital skills, and business. Build expertise in communication and gain key skills in your chosen focus: management, economics, ethics, or business informatics. With hands-on projects and a mix of different fields, this Master lets you create your own path and choose from several language options.

## ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Applications to this Master's programme are assessed case by case.

Prerequisites in Communication and in the chosen option: basic courses in communication, research methods and statistics, plus 30 ECTS from the relevant Bachelor minor at the SES Faculty.

- → Bachelor degree from a Swiss university meeting all prerequisites: direct admission
- → Bachelor degree in communication or in the relevant option from a Swiss University of Applied Sciences (HES): admission to a preMaster's programme, comprising up to 30 ECTS in communication and up to 30 ECTS in the chosen option.
- → Other academic backgrounds: individual assessment determines for possible admission and catch-up courses. Admission for foreign degrees is not automatic.
- → Diplomas older than 8 years are not accepted.

Apply via MyUnifr if already enrolled at Unifr.
