## Transitional provisions 2025-2026

These transitional provisions concern students whose reference programme is in a study plan prior to 2025, namely:

- Specialised Master of Science in Digital Neuroscience (120 ECTS)

The following transitional provisions apply.

## Cybernetic Systems: Bridging Brains, Machines and society (SDN.00006):

- Students who have already made an attempt at exam of the course ' Data analytics in Python (I09.00013) ' and got an insufficient grade, can undergo the repetition exam of this course during the next exam session.
- Students who have not yet attempted the exam of the course ' Data analytics in Python (I09.00013) ' , must follow the course ' Cvbernetic Systems: Bridging Brains, Machines and society (SDN.00006) ' .

## Neural Decoding of EEG signals (L25.01822):

- Students who have already succeeded at exam of the course ' Advanced EEG workshop on Brain Decoding (L25.01590) ' do not have to take the course ' Neural Decoding of EEG signals (L25.01822) ' .
- Students who have not taken the course ' Advanced EEG workshop on Brain Decoding (L25.01590) ' , must follow the course ' Neural Decoding of EEG signals (L25.01822) ' .
