## Duration of studies

The maximum duration of studies is 8 semesters . The programme can be completed in 3 to 4 semesters , depending on the student's pace.

## Compensation principle

The Faculty applies a compensation principle in the evaluation of academic performance according to article 6 from the

Règlement du 27 septembre 2021 sur l'octroi des Master à la Faculté des sciences économiques et sociales et du management de l'Université de Fribourg , (https://webapps.unifr.ch/legal/fr/document/2870310).

Seminar papers and the Master thesis must be passed individually - they cannot be compensated.

A maximum of 9 ECTS credits at Bachelor's level may be taken at Master's level. Registration must be done by the exam delegate.
