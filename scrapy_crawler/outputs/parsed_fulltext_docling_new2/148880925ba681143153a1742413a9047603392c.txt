## Master of Arts International and European Business

## Programme Highlights

Discover a Master that opens doors worldwide: learn in English in Fribourg's bilingual setting, join intercultural teams with classmates from 20+ countries, and tackle real business challenges through case studies and projects. Benefit from guest lectures by managers, international study opportunities, and the chance to earn a prestigious double degree in Strasbourg or in Pavia. Shape your future as a global leader and gain the skills employers seek: strategic thinking, profound business knowledge, and leadership in an international context.

<!-- image -->

## Mobility

An exceptional opportunity to shape your Master's and strengthen your academic journey by spending one or two semesters abroad. Experience new cultures, expand your international network, and boost your CV with all credits obtained fully recognised.

## ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Proof of sufficient English language skills may be required by the Office for Admissions, see www. unifr.ch/admission, depending on the curriculum of the candidate.

- → Bachelor degree in Management from a Swiss university : Unconditional Admission.
- → Bachelor degree in another discipline or from a foreign university : admission is based on a full review of the application.

Applications must include a lettre of motivation in addition to the documents required by the University of Fribourg Admissions Office. For foreign degrees from outside the EU/EFTA, GMAT results are mandatory.

The minimum requirement is 60 ECTS in Management. Where admission is granted, the SES Faculty may require the completion of additional Bachelorlevel ECTS credits.

- → Bachelor degree in Business Administration from a Swiss University of Applied Sciences (HES): admission is possible with a standard supplement of 30 ECTS.

For special cases (e.g., foreign degrees or Bachelor's degrees obtained more than eight years ago), admission is not automatic. Admission decisions are made on a case-by-case basis.

Applicants who are enrolled at the University of Fribourg must submit their application via the MyUnifr portal.

## FURTHER INFO

Enjoy a flexible structure in Fribourg as well as electives to choose from the programme modules, other Faculty courses, and up to 9 ECTS from the University's offer.

## Double Degree Programmes

Master in International and European Business (Fribourg)

MSc In International and European Business (Strasbourg)

and Laurea Magistralis in International Business and Economics (Pavia)

<!-- image -->

<!-- image -->

The Faculty of Management, Economics and Social Sciences of the University of Fribourg in Switzerland together with the EM Strasbourg Business School in France and the University of Pavia in Italy have established two double degree programmes leading to the award of two university Master degrees upon completion of an integrated 120 ECTS programme, with one year in Fribourg and one year in Strasbourg or Pavia.

Studying in the double degree programme gives students valuable experience abroad, strong intercultural experience, the perspective of professors and lecturers from many different nations and both a Swiss diploma and a French or Italian diploma from top ranked institutions. With these integrated programmes, students can study one year in the bilingual city of Fribourg in Switzerland, adding the unique Swiss perspective, in an economically sound country that has recently been ranked as the most competitive nation in the world. They are given the opportunity to spend their second year at the business school of the largest university in France, Strasbourg, or at the Faculty of Economics of the University of Pavia, in Italy. The language of the programmes is English. Up to 18 ECTS can be taken in any other language.

## APPLICATION

Applications must be sent by 31 January to mobility-delegate-ses@unifr.ch and must include the following documents:

- → Curriculum Vitae
- → Transcripts of BA and eventually MA marks
- → Letter of motivation

The Admissions Delegate will make a decision, in consultation with the double degree academic advisor, after the publication of the MA marks in February for a start at the partner university the following Autumn Semester.
