Validity period

The Double Degree programme between the University of Pavia and the University of Fribourg enables students to obtain two university Master's degrees - one from each institution - upon completion of an integrated 120 ECTS programme.

Students from Fribourg must complete 60 ECTS from coursework during their first year in Fribourg. In their second year, they study in Pavia, where they are required to complete 36 ECTS from coursework and 24 ECTS for a Master's thesis under the supervision of a professor from the University of Pavia.

## IMPORTANT NOTE

The Master's Degree Regulations apply fully and are legally binding in all circumstances. The related information outlined below (drawn from articles 6 and 7) is provided for guidance purposes only, to facilitate understanding, but does not replace the official regulations and has no legal value. In case of doubt, the official text of the regulations shall prevail ( Règlement du 27 septembre 2021 sur l'octroi des Master à la Faculté des sciences économiques et sociales et du management de l'Université de Fribourg , available at unifr.ch/ses/fr/etudes/reglements.html. N.B.: at the University of Fribourg, only regulations in French or German have legal force).

## Duration of studies

The maximum duration of studies is 8 semesters . The programme can be completed in 3 to 4 semesters , depending on the student's pace.

## Compensation principle

The Faculty applies a compensation principle in the evaluation of academic performance according to article 6 from the

Règlement du 27 septembre 2021 sur l'octroi des Master à la Faculté des sciences économiques et sociales et du management de l'Université de Fribourg , (https://webapps.unifr.ch/legal/fr/document/2870310).

Seminar papers and the Master thesis must be passed individually - they cannot be compensated.

For this programme at Fribourg the compensation is based on 60 total ECTS in courses assessments.

## Courses in Fribourg

Students are required to validate at least two core courses from the 'European and Global Business' (EUGB) module.

In addition, students must choose one optional module and validate at least two of its core courses . Optional modules cover a variety of management areas, allowing students to specialise according to their academic interests and career goals.
